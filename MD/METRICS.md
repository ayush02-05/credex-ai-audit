# Credex AI Audit – Key Metrics

## North Star Metric

**Number of Qualified Leads Generated (QLG) per Month**

### Why?

For a B2B lead-gen tool like Credex AI Audit, the most important outcome is generating qualified leads—users who complete an audit and provide their contact information to unlock the full report. This metric directly reflects the tool’s ability to attract, engage, and convert target users into sales opportunities.

## Input Metrics

1. **Audit Completions**  
   Number of users who finish entering their AI stack and generate a savings report. Drives QLG by indicating engagement and value delivery.
2. **Report Unlock Rate**  
   Percentage of audits where users submit their email and company info to access the full report. Measures conversion from interest to lead.
3. **Unique Organizations Audited**  
   Number of distinct companies (by domain) using the tool. Indicates reach and market penetration.

## What to Instrument First

- Audit creation and completion events (track funnel drop-off)
- Report unlock submissions (capture lead info)
- Company domain extraction (deduplicate leads)

## Pivot Trigger

If the monthly Qualified Leads Generated (QLG) falls below 10 for two consecutive months, it signals insufficient product-market fit or ineffective acquisition. This would trigger a pivot—either in product positioning, feature set, or go-to-market strategy.

---

**Note:** Metrics like DAU/MAU are not suitable for this tool, as B2B audit products are typically used infrequently but must deliver high value per use. Focus on lead generation and conversion efficiency.
